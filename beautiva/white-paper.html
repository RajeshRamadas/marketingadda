1. Executive summary

Beautiva proposes a platform that helps customers discover suitable beauty professionals, understand a proposed service and move from consultation to a completed appointment.

Professional expertise can be difficult to discover and compare. A salon may have capable staff and loyal customers while portfolios, prices, enquiries, quotations and bookings remain distributed across walk-ins, calls, messaging and social media.

The proposed journey is:

Discover expertise → explore a look → confirm feasibility and price → book → complete the service → return.

Customers should gain a clearer choice. Owners should gain useful visibility and a measurable connection between enquiries and completed business. Professionals should gain better presentation of specialisms and authentic work.

A proposed adjustable 5–6 km starting search radius could help customers find expertise beyond their immediate neighbourhood. Skill, availability, budget and travel time also matter. Distance does not guarantee enquiries, travel or bookings.

Beautiva enters a competitive market. Booking platforms already connect discovery with appointments. Salon systems support established operations. Beauty-technology companies offer visualisation capabilities. Service marketplaces compete for demand, while maps, social platforms and messaging remain familiar alternatives.

The investment case therefore depends on demonstrated execution: relevant matching, reliable consultation, manageable staff effort and improved economics for a defined segment.

The starting commercial hypothesis is ₹1,499 per location per month, up to five staff profiles and ten successful AI previews. A proposed ₹499 pack adds 25 previews. Costs and willingness to pay remain unverified.

The next milestone is a controlled local pilot followed by longer commercial follow-up to measure renewal at the standard price.

2. India's beauty-service opportunity

A diverse provider landscape

India includes neighbourhood salons, independent stylists, bridal specialists, home-based providers, home-service businesses and organised chains. Requirements vary by locality, language, service, price and customer expectations.

A neighbourhood salon may prioritise repeat business and scheduling. A bridal specialist may depend on portfolios, trials, travel and event dates. An independent stylist may work across locations.

Begin with a defined segment and locality.

What "unorganised" means here

Fragmentation refers to information and coordination gaps, not a lack of expertise or professionalism.

Digitisation can organise records and workflows. It does not automatically establish registration, accreditation, tax compliance or service safety.

Operating gap Consequence Proposed response
Expertise is difficult to compare Choices use incomplete information Profiles, specialisms and authentic portfolios
Prices and scope need explanation Enquiries stall or expectations differ Menus and an approved final quotation
Desired looks are hard to communicate Longer or unclear consultations References and supported visual consultation
Bookings sit across channels Changes and appointments are harder to track Confirmed appointments and service records
Repeat visits depend on memory Follow-up is inconsistent Permission-based reminders and history
Performance is measured informally Marketing value is difficult to assess Enquiry-to-completion and contribution reporting

Market sizing

National beauty spending is not automatically Beautiva's addressable market. Product sales, salon services, home services and software subscriptions are different categories.

Use:

Reachable salons × realistic paid adoption × annual realised subscription revenue.

Inputs need a verified local census, interviews, onboarding capacity and observed renewal. Estimate add-ons separately.

For scale illustration only, 100 locations paying ₹1,499 would produce ₹1,49,900 in monthly subscription billing before discounts and applicable taxes. This is arithmetic, not a forecast or existing traction.

This edition quotes no unverified national market value, salon count, growth rate or unorganised percentage.

3. The proposed Beautiva service

Professional-led discovery

Search should support specialism, authentic work, service category, budget, availability, location, travel time, accessibility and relevant language preferences.

A customer seeking a particular bridal style or nail service may value demonstrated expertise more than the nearest shop.

Verification must match the claim. Identity verification is not technical accreditation, and portfolios should not imply unestablished qualifications.

Visual consultation

Reference images and eligible AI previews could help customers explain preferences. Their purpose is a practical discussion about what is achievable.

Condition, product, lighting and technique affect the actual result. The professional confirms feasibility, duration, scope and final price before booking.

Connected service record

The proposed record connects:

Preference → reference/preview → professional assessment → approved quotation → appointment → completed service → feedback.

Measure whether it reduces repeated explanation and improves understanding, staff effort and outcomes.

Fit existing operations. Replacing mature billing, inventory or payroll systems should require demonstrated value. Integrations or manual handoffs need clear responsibilities to avoid duplicate records and bookings.

Product status

This is proposed commercial scope. Current implementation was not audited for this edition. Features enter a paid package only after documented acceptance.

5. Discovery beyond immediate walk-ins

Expertise may attract customers beyond regular walk-in catchments. An adjustable 5–6 km starting radius is a hypothesis to test by service, traffic, appointment value and willingness to travel.

A customer may accept a longer journey for a specialist or bridal trial and prefer convenience for a routine service.

Measure:

Relevant discovery → profile visit → qualified enquiry → confirmed booking → completed paid service → repeat visit.

Impressions do not establish revenue or incremental business. Compare distance bands, channels and services, including weak enquiries and travel-related cancellation.

Strong direct overlap

Fresha and other discovery/booking platforms are important benchmarks. Profiles, bookings, records and location search are not inherently unique.

Beautiva proposes a more specific journey: relevant skill, understanding an achievable service, professional approval and completed fulfilment. That proposition requires current competitor review and direct testing.

Incumbent advantages

Established alternatives may have distribution, recognition, operating experience, breadth, integrations and research investment.

No supplied evidence establishes that Beautiva can outperform them. A focused launch reduces scope; it does not remove acquisition, retention or quality requirements.

Potential technology suppliers

Visual vendors can be evaluated as suppliers as well as alternatives. Building every model internally can raise cost and delay acceptance. Licensing may allow concentration on workflow and fulfilment.

Evaluate quality, commercial rights, data handling, reliability and effective cost per usable output. No vendor price is assumed in this paper.

10. Illustrative salon business case

Contribution means service revenue less incremental service-delivery costs. It is distinct from revenue and net profit.

Assume 100 eligible qualified consultations monthly, 40% baseline completion, ₹780 contribution per additional standard-service booking, ₹300 per additional paid add-on and ₹1,998 software cost: subscription plus one pack.

The package provides 35 selectively used previews, not one for every consultation.

Monthly scenario Low Base High
Booking-rate increase 0 pp +5 pp +10 pp
Additional completed bookings 0 5 10
New-booking contribution ₹0 ₹3,900 ₹7,800
Additional add-ons among 40 baseline bookings 0 2 4
Additional add-on contribution ₹0 ₹600 ₹1,200
Total added contribution ₹0 ₹4,500 ₹9,000
After ₹1,998 software charge −₹1,998 ₹2,502 ₹7,002

pp means percentage points. Count only add-ons above existing business. Apply them to the original 40 bookings to avoid double counting with new appointments.

These are scenarios, not forecasts or results.

Three additional bookings at ₹780 cover software charges alone. Extra consultation time, acquisition, messaging, discounts and capacity can raise break-even.

Displaced appointments are not incremental growth. Do not monetise time savings twice if resulting additional appointments are already counted.

11. Research foundation and validation

Published research

Augmented reality overlays digital elements on a view of the real world. Generative systems can create or alter imagery. Evidence about one does not automatically transfer to the other.

Selected research examines online service experiences (Hilken et al. [2]), consumer responses to AR (Javornik [3]), experience, satisfaction and willingness to buy (Poushneh and Vasquez-Parraga [4]), and retail AR and sales (Tan et al. [5]).

This is a rationale for testing, not proof of Beautiva's quality, Indian salon outcomes or willingness to pay. This narrative synthesis is not a systematic review or a peer-reviewed Beautiva study.

Separate discovery and consultation effects

Discovery evaluation tracks relevant traffic, enquiries, completed appointments and repeat behaviour by source and distance.

Last-touch attribution does not establish that a customer would not otherwise have visited. Later geographic or phased-rollout comparisons can investigate incrementality.

Consultation evaluation compares eligible consultations using the usual process with a Beautiva-assisted process. It assesses understanding, staff effort, quotation and completion among customers entering the measured workflow.

A bundled comparison does not isolate AI. A later study could compare usual consultation, structured consultation without AI and structured consultation with an eligible preview.

Proposed pilot

Begin with approximately five salons in one locality, subject to readiness. Collect baseline information before intervention, run an approximately eight-week operational pilot and continue commercial follow-up.

Where feasible, randomise eligible periods or appointments. Prespecify eligibility, services, outcome windows and exclusions.

Primary consultation outcome:

Completed paid appointments ÷ eligible qualified consultations.

Analyse by assigned workflow and report actual usage separately.

Supporting measure Purpose
Understanding of scope, quotation and preview limits Decision clarity
Defects, failures and retries Technical reliability
Consultation and support time Operating burden
Contribution and incremental costs Commercial value
Complaints, cancellations and refunds Expectation mismatch
Initial payment and renewal Willingness to pay
Discovery outcomes by source/distance Wider visibility relevance

Account for service mix, capacity, promotions, staff learning, repeated customers and within-salon correlation. Retain refusals, failures and missing records.

A five-salon pilot is operational and unlikely to establish modest uplift reliably. Use observed variability to design a later adequately powered study.

Longer renewal follow-up

Eight weeks cannot establish standard-price renewal with a 14-day trial and three discounted paid months.

Continue through the first ₹1,499 billing cycle and subsequent renewal or cancellation where feasible. Separate intention from actual payment.

12. Product quality and service trust

Visual reliability

Missing a nail, spilling onto skin or changing an unintended feature can undermine consultation.

Reported live-nail issues have not been revalidated for this edition. Modes without a documented pass should remain outside the initial paid scope.

Dimension Evaluation
Nail coverage Annotated nail area covered, for every visible nail
Skin spill Painted area outside the nail; assess thumbs separately
Position and scale Input sizes, orientation, mirrored displays and poses
Motion stability Edge jitter, dropouts and coverage change
Perceptual quality Actual before-and-after browser captures
Usable output Accepted quality, not merely API success

Use photos/nails_classic_nude-front.jpg, other bundled nail photos, stationary hands and moving-hand sequences. Contrasting blue polish should reveal missed regions and spill.

A hand-wide average can hide an entirely unpainted finger. Report per-nail outcomes and failures. Prespecify numerical criteria. Regression passes alone are insufficient.

Honest presentation

Distinguish generated previews from actual work. A professional confirms feasibility and quotation. Portfolios need permission; reviews and badges should explain what was checked.

Data controls

Explain image purpose, processing, storage, access, retention and deletion. Treat service use, marketing and model training appropriately separately.

Do not automatically share salon customer lists. Review applicable Indian privacy, consumer, payment, tax and platform requirements before launch.

The pre-pilot case is a programme for establishing evidence.

Commercial refinement Price, allowance and support Standard-price renewal and repeatable onboarding

Funding should support reliable modes, supply and demand, onboarding, support, measurement and data controls.

Set amount and runway from an operating budget. No valuation, raise amount or traction is assumed.

Distinguish salon from customer acquisition, upfront onboarding from recurring delivery, introductory from standard revenue, and revenue from contribution. Lifetime-value claims need retention evidence.

Investor positioning

Beautiva is developing a professional-led journey from local discovery to consultation and completed service. Its proposed opportunity is easier skill discovery and clearer decisions.

The next milestone is accepted quality, manageable staff effort, actual cost and sustained paid use in a focused Indian locality.

Salon positioning

Beautiva is being developed to help suitable customers discover your team's expertise and understand the service before booking.

The proposed workflow connects profile, authentic work, supported consultation and approved quotation. A small pilot would measure added value after software, staff effort and other costs, with clear terms and realistic expectations.
