Questions

What businesses ask us

We already have our own app. Why Beautiva?

Keep it. Beautiva brings customers who don't know you yet, and AI try-on that your app doesn't have.

Who owns the customer data?

Each salon or branch keeps its bookings, chats, customer notes and reviews. We never sell customer data, and customer photos are not used to train AI.

Do you take a commission on bookings?

No. Listing is free, and Premium is a flat monthly fee per salon or branch. Customers pay you at the visit.

What if the AI look isn't possible on their hair?

The stylist card and quote let you say what's achievable, with the price and time, before the visit. The customer accepts or declines, so expectations are set in writing.

Does it work with our billing software?

It runs beside it today, and head office can download every branch's numbers as CSV. Tell us which software you use; a direct link is on our list.

Our staff are too busy for another app.

Answering a request is one tap, and training takes 30 minutes per branch. Requests can be accepted automatically if you prefer.

Which cities?

We are starting with salons, artists and chains in Bangalore and growing across India. Get in touch from any city.
