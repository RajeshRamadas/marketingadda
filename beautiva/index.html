FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Beautiva free?

Yes, you can download Beautiva, browse every style, get your face analysis and book salons for free. You also get 3 free looks every month on the styles marked Free to try. For more, credit packs start at $0.99 for 7 looks.

Do I need an account?

Yes, to use the app. Sign in with an email code, Google or Apple, so there is no password to remember. To look around first, the browser preview gives you 2 free looks a day without an account.

How real do the try-ons look?

Beautiva uses AI to put the style on your own photo while keeping your face, features and skin tone. It is a preview to help you decide, so your stylist’s final result may differ a little.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, front-facing selfie in good, even light, with your hair visible and nothing covering your face.

Is it only for women?

No. Men get haircuts, fades, beards, moustaches and hair colour, and you can choose to see styles for women, men or everyone.

What happens to my photos?

They are used only to make your looks and face analysis. You can delete them, or your whole account, from the app. Read the privacy policy for details.

I run a salon or work as an artist. How do I get listed?

Download the app, sign in and open My listing from your Profile to create your page. Our team reviews it before it goes live.
